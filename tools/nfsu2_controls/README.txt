==============================================================================
NFSU2 Controls Manager for R36H / R36S (playOS / ArkOS)
==============================================================================

DESCRIPTION:
  NFSU2 Controls is an interactive, native ncurses TUI utility designed to
  customize and remap gamepad buttons for Need for Speed: Underground 2 (Recompiled).

FEATURES:
  1. Quick Presets:
     - 1 PS2 Classic: Authentic PS2 console control layout for R36H (B=Gas, Y=Brake, A=Handbrake, X=Camera/GPS).
     - 2 Modern Trigger: Gas on R2 Trigger, Brake on L2 Trigger, Handbrake on A, Nitro on R1.
  2. Full Custom Remapper:
     - 3 Custom: Individually reassign buttons for 9 driving actions (Throttle, Brake, E-Brake,
       Nitrous, Shift Up/Down, Look Back, Camera, Reset Car).
  3. Factory Reset:
     - 4 Reset: Instant 1-click restore to clean default, clearing custom controller overrides.

INSTALLATION (PortMaster Flat Standard):
  Copy the following files directly into your Ports directory (/roms/ports/):
    - NFSU2_Controls.sh
    - images/NFSU2_Controls-thumb.png
    - nfsu2controls/ (auto-hidden subfolder)

USAGE:
  Launch "NFSU2 Controls" directly from your handheld's PORTS menu.
  - Navigate options using D-Pad Up / Down.
  - Press A or Enter to select/edit.
  - Press B or ESC to go back or exit.
